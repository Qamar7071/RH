# RH Labs: Company Information

## About
- RH Labs is a software house based in Hostel City, Islamabad, Pakistan. Founded in 2026.
- We build AI chatbots, AI agents, and websites / web apps for businesses in Pakistan and abroad.
- We are a small team on purpose: clients talk directly to the people who build their project.
- We work in English and Urdu.
- We work online with clients anywhere, and can meet clients in Islamabad in person.

## Services

### 1. AI Chatbots
- Chatbots for websites and WhatsApp that answer customer questions 24/7.
- Trained on the client's own products, prices, policies and FAQs, so answers are accurate.
- Can collect leads (name, phone, email), take bookings and appointments, and hand the chat to a human staff member when needed.
- Good for shops, online stores, clinics, schools, hotels, restaurants and service businesses.

### 2. AI Agents
- AI agents that do multi-step work for a team: reading documents and emails, updating Google Sheets or a CRM, sending follow-ups and preparing reports and summaries.
- Can answer questions from a company's own documents (document Q&A / RAG).
- We first map the client's workflow, then automate the slowest and most repetitive parts.
- Good for offices, agencies, sales teams and operations teams.

### 3. Websites & Web Apps
- Business websites, portfolio websites, landing pages and web apps with login, dashboards and admin panels.
- Every website is mobile-friendly and fast.
- We help with domain, hosting and basic SEO setup.
- A website can be combined with an AI chatbot.

## Typical timelines
- Simple website: 1 to 3 weeks.
- AI chatbot: 1 to 3 weeks.
- AI agent: 2 to 6 weeks, depending on how many systems it connects to.
- The exact deadline is written in the quote before work starts.

## Pricing

## How we work (process)
1. Free consultation: we learn about the business and what it needs.
2. Written quote: features, price and deadline agreed on paper before work starts.
3. Design and build: we show a working demo every week so the client can give feedback early.
4. Launch: we put it live, train the client's team and hand over the code, logins and documentation.
5. Support: bug fixes, small updates, and optional monthly maintenance.

## Payment
- Payment terms and payment method are written in the quote before any work starts.

## Ownership
- After the project is complete, the client owns the code and their data. We hand over everything.

## Support and maintenance
- After launch we fix bugs and make small updates.
- Monthly maintenance plans are available for websites, chatbots and agents.
