Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information RH Labs ("we", "us") collects through this website, why we collect it, and how you can contact us about it. RH Labs is a software house based in Hostel City, Islamabad, Pakistan.

Information we collect

  • Project request form: your name, email address, company (optional), the service you need, budget range, deadline and project details.
  • Chat assistant: the messages you type into the chat assistant and its replies.
  • Technical information: your IP address and browser type, which our server records to prevent spam and abuse.
  • Visitor statistics: if enabled, we use privacy-friendly analytics that count page visits without advertising cookies and without building a profile of you.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and deliver our services.
  • To answer questions in the chat assistant and improve its answers.
  • To protect the website from spam and misuse.
  • To understand how many people visit the website and which pages are useful.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Chat assistant and AI

The chat assistant uses an AI service provider to write replies. The messages you send in the chat are passed to that provider to generate an answer. Please do not share passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information in the chat. For anything private, contact us directly on WhatsApp or email.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the website, and only as needed: website and server hosting, email delivery, the AI provider for the chat assistant, and appointment booking if you choose to book a call. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep project requests and chat messages for as long as we need them to work with you and for our business records, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you book a call, the booking service may use its own cookies under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Email us and we will reply within a reasonable time.

Children

Our services are for businesses. This website is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
